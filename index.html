KNOWLEDGE BASE

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions regarding our IT support, software development cycles, and hardware warranties.

What is your guaranteed response time for IT emergencies?

For Critical and Emergency SLA clients, our technical dispatch team acknowledges and initiates remediation within 15 minutes, 24 hours a day, 7 days a week. For standard tickets, response is guaranteed within 1 to 2 hours.

Do you provide on-site Windows installation and hardware setups?

Yes! We offer both remote deployment and on-site engineering visits. We install clean, genuine Windows OS, configure active directory credentials, deploy endpoint antivirus protection, and configure printers and network peripherals.

What warranty comes with your certified laptops and hardware?

Every certified laptop undergoes a rigorous 35-point hardware diagnostic test and comes with an official 6-month to 1-year replacement warranty, battery health certification (>85%), and free lifetime technical support.

How does Google Business Profile setup benefit my company?

A verified and optimized Google Business Profile ensures your business appears prominently on Google Maps and local search results, driving high-intent customer calls, building review reputation, and establishing verified authenticity.

What software development tech stacks do you specialize in?

We specialize in modern, high-performance stacks including React, Next.js, Node.js, Python/Django, TypeScript, PostgreSQL, Docker, AWS, Cloudflare, and native/cross-platform mobile app development.
